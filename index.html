Initialed Endee (M.i.E)

Initialed Endee (also known as M.i.E) is a Cape Town-based recording artist, producer, and creative director leading Initialed Entertainment. Merging corporate media strategy with raw artistic expression, he turns ambitious creative concepts into polished, release-ready experiences.

A two-time Red Bull 64 Bars performer, Endee commands the mic with precision and presence. His catalog—spanning from his early LiL kid sessions to the Forbidden Heart EP and singles like Lightwork and Eternal (Free$tyle)—is built on deep collaboration across the South African hip-hop circuit. Currently, he is locked in with producer and DJ MDP, crafting some of his best work to date alongside upcoming joint releases. His broader discography features collaborations with King Klutch (From the Kaap, Feisty), Nexus ZA and Swag4TheBagBeats (Drip Check), Billy Brvndxne (Senorita), Dub.L.C (You), Boast NDK (GSh!t), Awakened Records ZA (Lightwork), Jade Alonzo (Half Full, ABC), TheOneAndOnly DMG, and SCXTT3RBRXIN MC (lowkey).

Visual storytelling is equally central to his brand. Endee regularly partners with acclaimed Cape Town sports, culture, and live-event cinematographer ShotByRafeeq (Rafeeq September) alongside frequent musical and visual collaborator Jade Alonzo—supported by VOS Media, Nathmie Davids, JWD Photography, and Hybrid Creativity—to translate his sound into high-energy music videos and visual campaigns.

Beyond his own artistry, Initialed Endee runs a full-service creative agency built to elevate independent talent and forward-thinking brands through studio engineering, beat production, visual direction, web design, and brand execution.
